# CLYFAR: The Name

**CLYFAR** = Welsh word meaning **"clever"**

Not an acronym! 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁷󠁬󠁳󠁿

---

## Why "Clyfar"?

The name reflects the system's approach to winter ozone forecasting:

1. **Clever use of fuzzy logic** - Expert knowledge encoded in 6 simple rules
2. **Clever ensemble processing** - 31 GEFS members treated as analog scenarios  
3. **Clever possibility theory** - Acknowledges what we don't know (subnormal distributions)
4. **Clever thresholding** - Users can tune POD/FAR trade-off (moderate≥0.3 achieves 72% POD, 0% FAR)

The Welsh naming also connects to the cultural heritage of careful observation and adaptation to weather in mountainous regions.

---

## Pronunciation

**CLYFAR**: /ˈklɪvar/ (roughly "KLUH-var")

Similar to English "clever" but with Welsh pronunciation.
